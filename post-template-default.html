Intro

Hi everyone,

Welcome to the very first edition of Her Match.

This newsletter started from a simple realization.

I enjoy watching women's sports, but I kept missing games and tournaments—not because I wasn't interested, but because I simply didn't know they were happening. Unlike men's sports, where upcoming matches are constantly promoted through news, television, and social media, following women's sports often means searching across multiple websites and league calendars.

I figured I couldn't be the only one.

So I'm building Her Match: a simple weekly newsletter that brings together what's happening across women's sports in one place, making it easier to discover competitions, matches, and tournaments.

This is a test edition that I'm sharing with friends and family before launching publicly. I'd love your honest feedback.
